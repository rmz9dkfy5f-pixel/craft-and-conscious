Our craft

Designed for interiors, built for rituals.

Craft Candle Company started with one simple idea: scent should feel like part of your space, not an afterthought. Each candle is treated like a design object—inside and out.

We work in small seasonal runs, blending fragrance in-house and pouring by hand in our studio. Every pour is tested for hot throw, burn quality, and how it behaves in an actual room—not just a lab.

Clean-burning coconut-soy wax blend with cotton wicks.
Phthalate-free, paraben-free fragrance oils sourced from trusted houses.
Reusable glass vessels designed to live on as objects in your home.
Limited releases each season to keep the line intentional and focused.
