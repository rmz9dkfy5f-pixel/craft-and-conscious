How the scents are organized

Craft Candle Company is built around how you actually move through a day — not just top/heart/base notes.

Core Rituals

The everyday set. Scents built to live on your desk, nightstand, or kitchen counter without ever getting old.

Think: calm focus, soft mornings, and “I always burn this when I’m working.”

City Stories

Candles that feel like late-night slices, subway tile, and favorite side streets. Slightly moodier, a little louder.

Great for living rooms, media corners, and playlists that lean left-of-center.

Evening Rituals

Deep, slow-burning scents made for unwinding: reading, journaling, stretching, or just existing.

Best for the hour before bed, or the one after everyone else falls asleep.

Story

Built around how real people live.

Craft Candle Company

This studio started as a way to make spaces feel more intentional without redecorating an entire room. One match, one scent, one small ritual.

  • Coconut-apricot wax blend for a smooth, even burn.
  • Fragrance oils vetted for safer burning (phthalate-free, no weird extras).
  • Small pours so each batch can be tested, tweaked, and improved.
  • Amber glass jars that look good in real homes, not just photo shoots.

First-cup candle.

Deep work timer.

End-of-day page.
