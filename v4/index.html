How we work with you
From first idea to finished, shelf-ready candles.
  • 1. Quick intro call.

    We suggest scent families and pour test samples for you to burn in your own space.

    Jar style, wax color, label layout, and any small touches that make it feel like your brand.
  • 4. Production & reorder rhythm.
    We set realistic timelines and reorder minimums so you can stay in stock without overbuying.
