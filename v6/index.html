How this grows into a full line
From test jars on a bedspread to a polished brand on shelves.
  • Dial in the scent profiles.
    Use these early pours to decide which directions feel most “Craft and Conscious” — cozy, smoky, fresh, or something in between.
  • Lock in vessel + wick choices.
    Amber glass, wood wicks, and clean labels already create a strong aesthetic foundation.
  • Design labels that match the logo.
    Tie in the CC mark, your flame icon, and minimal typography for a cohesive shelf presence.
  • Scale into collections.
    Group scents into families (cozy, fresh, woody, floral) for easy merchandising online and in-store.
